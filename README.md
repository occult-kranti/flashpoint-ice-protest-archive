# FLASHPOINT — ICE Protest–Police Clashes, 2024–2026

A sourced, filterable incident archive of every verified clash between anti-ICE protesters and
law enforcement in the United States from January 2024 through September 2026 — riot-control
weapons used, arrests, injuries, escalation days — plus a curated directory of the information
ecosystem covering the protests (subreddits, Instagram handles, journalists, partisan and
independent channels, and on-the-ground content capturers).

## Pages

- `index.html` — the incident archive: 79 verified incidents, timeline, filters (year, cluster,
  last-6-months, search), per-incident source lists (news / video / Reddit / official).
- `sources.html` — the source ecosystem directory, organized by city and category.

## Method

Multi-agent research swarm (170+ searches, scholar + web), cross-verified, with a
skeptic-loop adversarial review skill applied to the academic literature. Reddit links are
marked anecdotal; every incident is corroborated by news reporting. Compiled September 2026.

## Files

All data is embedded in the HTML pages (no build step, no dependencies).
